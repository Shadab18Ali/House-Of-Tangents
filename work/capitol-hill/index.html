We helped a 22-year-old hotel show its experience online.

Overview

Capitol Hill already had regular guests and local trust when we began working together. The hotel had built that reputation over 22 years, but its digital presence didn’t reflect enough of what people experienced there.

We worked across the hotel and its outlets, including Yellow Sapphire and Floats. Aura and banquet communication also formed part of the brief. Our content gave people a clearer view of the food, ambience, service and reasons to visit.

For Aura, we developed the rooftop’s logo and launch communication, giving the new experience its own introduction within the group.

The challenge

A 22-year-old hotel with regular guests and local trust, but a digital presence that didn’t reflect enough of what people experienced there.

The tangent

Show the food, ambience, service and events people were already experiencing, and give each outlet and the new Aura rooftop a clearer place online.

Execution and results

We made the property’s offerings easier to discover.

We brought more consistency to social communication and Google Business Profile. The content helped people remember the property and explore what it offered, while regular updates made the information easier to check.

The hotel’s dining and rooftop offerings needed to feel connected to its established reputation. Event communication mattered too. We used content planning, visual storytelling, timely updates and campaign creative to give those experiences a clearer place online.

More people saw the hotel and looked for its location.

Organic account reach grew by more than 800% within the first two months. After about 2.5 months, taps on the business address increased by up to 400%.

Google Business Profile recorded 2,873 interactions in one reporting period, a 54.2% increase against the previous month. A different period recorded 5,363 interactions, up 25% against its comparison period.

Those actions showed people checking the property and looking for its location.
