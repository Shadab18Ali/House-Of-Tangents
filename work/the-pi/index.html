Introduction

The Pi needed a brand people could recognise before they opened the app. We developed the original logo and identity. We also built the website and created its social presence, then carried that work into launch material and giveaways.

At XISS, the team supported the product introduction and demonstrations.

We also designed cups and desk accessories, including mouse-pad designs, diaries, shirts, T-shirts and giveaway packaging. The identity needed to hold together whether people met it on a screen or took it back to their desk.
