Overview

Hotel AVN Grand already had an established local presence and business bookings. We worked on how its offerings appeared online, giving people more ways to discover the property as the hospitality market expanded.

The group engagement covered social media and shoots, editing, website work, Google profiles and advertising. We kept the hotel’s established audience in mind while creating space for its dining and café offerings.

Kalinga India and The Lobby Café formed part of the same engagement. Their communication needed to explain why someone would visit each place while keeping the relationship with the hotel clear.

The challenge

An established local hotel needed its offerings, including dining and café, to be easier to find online.
