Overview

Navdurga Business Park was a planned development, so the usual approach of filming the completed property wasn’t available. The client also wanted something more interesting than familiar business-park promotion.

We approached the location through a visitor’s experience. We imagined arriving at the airport and travelling towards the site, with the journey introducing nearby cities and connections along the way.

AI generation let us create those scenes before there was a finished location to film. It gave the proposed development a story people could follow.

The challenge

A planned development with no finished property to film, and a client who wanted something more interesting than familiar business-park promotion.

The tangent

Approach the location through a visitor’s experience, from the airport to the site, and use AI generation to show the planned park before it exists.

Execution and results

We built an AI walkthrough of the proposed facilities.

The facilities film used a fully AI-generated FPV drone-style walkthrough. It moved through the planned park to show cranes and internal road connectivity, alongside backhoe loaders, weighbridges, warehouses and loading trucks.

We handled the concept and scene planning, scripts, prompt writing, AI generation, voiceovers, sound and editing. Full page management also covered content planning and captions, followed by organic publishing.

We completed and posted the films, and the client loved the approach. No ads ran on these videos.

Note: The visuals present proposed facilities rather than filmed footage of completed construction.
