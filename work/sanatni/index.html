Overview

The work began with the earlier Blissful Life project. We helped develop Sanatni’s name and logo, along with its visual language, guidelines and messaging.

Bhakti was central to the platform. The communication also made room for inner peace and spiritual connection.

The product brief included a conversational AI experience connected with Shri Sadguru Riteshwarji Maharaj. We worked alongside developers on the website and app journeys, with communication and onboarding forming part of our scope.

We also handled social strategy, scripts, shoots, editing, advertising, launch assets and merchandise.

The challenge

Introduce a spiritual platform built around bhakti, inner peace and a conversational AI experience.

The tangent

Carry one consistent identity into a large public launch in Ayodhya and into direct conversations with the people attending.

Execution and results

The Sanatni launch brought us to Ayodhya.

For the January 2025 launch in Ayodhya, we worked on promotional assets and posters, signage communication, content capture, audience interaction and onboarding. Awareness advertising supported the platform’s introduction at the large public gathering.

The work brought the product into a spiritual setting and into direct conversations with the people attending. We kept the identity consistent across those encounters.
