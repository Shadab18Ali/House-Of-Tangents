Introduction

Our work with Dense began with video editing. The owner liked the edits and asked us to take on the wider marketing. The product gave us a clear starting point: humongous sandwiches with generous fillings.

We developed the content around that appetite appeal, using the names and copy alongside design and product footage. One line captured the approach: “Watching won’t fill you up. We will.”

Dense is a cloud kitchen, so we brief local photographers and videographers remotely, then work with their material. Our team doesn’t shoot the food itself.
