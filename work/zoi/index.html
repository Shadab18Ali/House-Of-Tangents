Life at ZOI

Overview

ZOI was already operating when we joined in May 2025. The brief was to support revenue and attract stronger guest interest, while giving people reasons to return. We connected the venue’s boho atmosphere with food and events, backed by offers and useful content.

We handled brand communication, social pages, shoots, scripts, editing, website, menus, monthly food highlights, enquiries and paid campaigns. Seasonal promotions, including a sushi festival, brought fresh reasons to visit.

Approvals involved several management stakeholders. We helped the team work through designated points of contact so decisions could move more consistently.

The challenge

ZOI was already operating, and the brief was to support revenue, attract stronger guest interest and give people reasons to return.

The tangent

Connect the venue’s boho atmosphere with food and events, then use guest data to keep the conversation going after a visit.

Execution and results

Events gave the venue reasons to keep talking.

We named and promoted The Gin Affair, including offer communication and venue material, with standees and table applications.

For Sundowner, we developed promotion from the teaser and trailer through coverage and an aftermovie. Ads and booking support formed part of that event work. Wednesday karaoke gave guests a regular reason to visit.

ZOI Care had a different purpose. We helped communicate access to pads and tampons in the washrooms, and created an awareness video with Dr Ashutosh, an orthopaedic surgeon.

Note: ZOI Care was a separate care initiative.

We helped the guest conversation continue after a visit.

Using Reelo, we helped connect venue visits with guest details and follow-up communication. Birthday wishes and relevant offers gave later messages a reason to arrive.

The team reported around five to ten enquiries a day and an increase in footfall.
