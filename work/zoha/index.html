Introduction

Zoha Jewellery gave us a brief with a Dhanteras and Diwali deadline. Over approximately a month, we planned and produced content for gold and diamond pieces, with temple jewellery also part of the campaign.

Our photographers shot the pieces. We combined that original photography with AI-assisted visual development, design and editing, keeping the jewellery’s detail central to the work.

We handled campaign strategy, social media, shoots, video editing, design and Meta awareness ads. Offers included flat 10% off diamond jewellery and up to 25% off gold making charges. The campaign received positive feedback and views. It also brought enquiries, while sales figures remained pending.
