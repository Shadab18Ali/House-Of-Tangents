Introduction

One upcoming property in Dhanbad needed four identities. We created original logos and guidelines for the parent property and for each of the three venues inside it.

The four identities

Parent property

Moksh

Moksh is the parent property in Dhanbad. We created its logo and brand guidelines as the foundation for the upcoming destination.

Pool-table café

Sora

Sora is the property’s pool-table café. We created its logo and guidelines, keeping its purpose distinct from the rooftop venue.

Rooftop venue

Solace

Solace is the rooftop venue. Its logo and guidelines establish its own identity within the property ahead of launch.

Hair salon

Sparrow

Sparrow is the hair salon. We created its logo and guidelines, then carried the identity into its service menu.

The challenge

One upcoming Dhanbad property needed four distinct identities: the building, a pool-table café, a rooftop venue and a hair salon.

Our approach

Give each venue its own identity under one parent property.

Delivered scope

  • Four original logos
  • Brand guidelines for each
  • Sparrow service menu

Outcome and status

Status: Identities developed. The property is upcoming, and websites and launch marketing remained future work.
