Brand & packaging

Shafaf

We gave a familiar product a new brand introduction.

Client
Shafaf
Sector
Brand & packaging
Status
Ongoing (Nov 2026)Identity and packaging developed. Online launch and marketing were still ahead.

Introduction

The client’s broom business has roots going back to 1960. Shafaf is a new brand preparing for a different market presence, including an online future.

We developed the logo and guidelines, alongside packaging and brand direction. Two creative routes considered tier-one and tier-two city customers, with quick commerce forming the other route to market.

The design explored the name’s cleaning association and details from the product itself. Broom-inspired lettering and range applications helped us work towards an identity people could recognise in a familiar category.

The challenge

A broom business with roots going back to 1960 was preparing a new brand for a different market, including an online future.

Our approach

Explore the name’s cleaning association and details from the product itself, with broom-inspired lettering.

How it came together

We worked through how Shafaf would appear on the pack.

Packaging and brand-application work considered phool and kaanti brooms. Colour directions used blue and red, along with yellow and green from the identity explorations.

Delivered scope

  • Logo
  • Guidelines
  • Packaging for phool and kaanti brooms
  • Brand direction

Outcome and status

Status: Identity and packaging developed. Online launch and marketing were still ahead.

Shafaf is a new brand. The underlying broom business was established in 1960.

Colour directions explored blue and red, along with yellow and green. Final production colours and the printed rollout follow approved artwork, not every route we explored.
