i-Fit.Me

Overview

i-Fit.Me brought healthcare and wellness into a broad product conversation. We created the website and social presence, supported by positioning, research and feature explanations.

Discussions explored appointments and doctor access, records and remote consultations through user needs. We wanted someone to understand where the product could fit into their life without having to learn the whole system in their first encounter.

The challenge

Make a broad healthcare and wellness proposition understandable to the different audiences it needed to reach.

The tangent

Explain where the product could fit into someone’s life without asking them to learn the whole system at first contact.
