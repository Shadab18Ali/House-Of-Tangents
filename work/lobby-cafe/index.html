Introduction

The Lobby Café joined the AVN Grand group during our shared engagement. We created its original logo and menu so it could introduce itself with a distinct identity.

The identity and menu helped give the café a place of its own in the group’s communication, rather than leaving people to discover it as an unnamed part of the hotel.

The challenge

Give a café inside a hotel group a place of its own in the group’s communication.
