Shambhavi Shiva

Shambhavi’s content needed to sound and feel like her.

Overview

Shambhavi Shiva already had a large following, but the content didn’t have a clear direction. We developed content pillars around her Bihari humour and spontaneous personality, while making room for her spiritual interests.

Bihari aunty and mother-in-law characters gave the comedy familiar situations to work with. We developed a series around the twelve Jyotirlingas for her spiritual journey. Her experience of life after divorce informed relevant stories without becoming the account’s only subject.

We handled strategy, scripts, shoots, editing, publishing and collaborations. Speaking material and presentations supported her public appearances too.

The challenge

A large following, but content without a clear direction.

The tangent

Build content pillars around her Bihari humour, recurring characters and spontaneous personality, with room for her spiritual interests.

Execution and results

We developed original shows around her ability to host.

JT20 Baithki with Shambhavi began as a conversation format featuring players and business personalities. We developed the concept and name. Guests included Saurabh Tiwary and Nadeem sir. The JSCA president also featured.

For Pitchside with Shambhavi, we developed a shorter interview format and created its show identity, logo animation, intros and covers. We published both formats in collaboration with JT20.

We also prepared speaking scripts for public appearances and BNI communication, with presentations when required.

The character-led approach helped more people find her.

Early posts struggled to attract a few hundred views. With a clearer direction around characters and personality, selected videos reached 11K and 15K views. Others reached 20K, and two videos exceeded one million views.

The wider personal-branding work also gave her opportunities beyond the grid, including interview formats, guest conversations, speaking material and partnerships.
