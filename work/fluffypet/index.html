Introduction

FluffyPet set out to bring pet-related services and information into one place. We joined the project at its beginning in November 2023, working on the logo and guidelines, website, content and early onboarding.

We organised provider outreach across Jharkhand and Kolkata. We spoke with vets and pet shops, day boarders, walkers and NGOs.

Beta use showed that providers found the app difficult to navigate. Some asked us to create their accounts for them. That feedback gave us a reason to pause the wider launch work.

The challenge

Bring pet-related services and information into one place.

The strategy

Take provider feedback back into the product: beta users found the app hard to navigate, so the wider launch was paused while the experience was revisited.

Creative execution

We took that feedback back into the product.

We mapped user journeys and reviewed screens and feature changes. We also refreshed the brand guidelines and coordinated revisions with developers.

Our work covered social shoots, videos, website communication, onboarding emails, email automation, campaign material and packaging/product applications, including reflective collars. No paid ads have run yet.

We helped adopters start using FluffyPet at the camp.

At a pet-adoption camp with Street Dogs of Ranchi, sponsored by FluffyPet and MARS, we helped adopters create profiles and understand the app. Posters and standees carried the line “Found your +1? Make it official.”

Rather than relying on a QR code to do all the explaining, we were there to help people take the next step.
