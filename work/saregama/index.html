Introduction

The shooting team brought us a behind-the-scenes edit for “Maavdi,” identified in the brief with artist Gurdeep Mehendi, for Saregama Music’s platforms. Their main requirement was clear: everyone involved in the room had to appear.

We selected footage to include the lighting and sound crews, as well as the people whose contributions are easier to miss. The project came through a photographer we’d worked with during JT20. We handled end-to-end post-production using the supplied footage.

The challenge

The shooting team required that everyone involved in the room appeared in the behind-the-scenes film.

Our approach

Let the people and the footage carry the edit, with restraint and no motion graphics.

How it came together

We let the people and the footage carry the edit.

We handled footage selection and editing, then worked on the colour grade. Sound and subtitles were part of the job too. The initial edit took approximately two days. Correction rounds and management review followed.

The footage called for restraint. Constant cuts or motion graphics would have drawn attention away from the people the video needed to include. We didn’t use motion graphics.

The shooting team gave outstanding feedback on the work.

Delivered scope

  • One BTS film with revision versions
  • Footage selection
  • Editing
  • Colour
  • Sound
  • Subtitles
