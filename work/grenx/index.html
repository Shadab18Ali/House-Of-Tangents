Overview

GRENX sells parts and accessories for Grenadier owners. We created its Shopify website and supported an exhibition giveaway with a connected follow-up journey.

We developed QR posters, standees, design elements, segmented forms, giveaway messages, email communication and automation flows. That gave an exhibition visitor somewhere useful to go after scanning, and gave the business a way to continue the conversation.

The client reported good entries and leads. The direct engagement has since continued into further automation and data-workflow projects.

The challenge

Connect an exhibition giveaway with a useful next step for each kind of visitor.

The tangent

Use segmented forms to tell existing owners from people with a vehicle on order, with a separate route for the wider audience.

Execution and results

We planned different follow-ups for different visitors.

QR-led creative introduced the giveaway and helped people enter. The forms could distinguish existing owners from people with a vehicle on order, with a separate route for the wider interested audience.

Email sequences and automation carried the communication beyond the exhibition. Workflow development included Resend and an OpenAI-powered agent. We connected the forms with the messages and automation flow, while further systems work continued.
