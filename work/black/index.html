Introduction

Choosing a salon means trusting someone with how you’ll look when you leave. For Black, we made the quality of the work easier to judge before someone booked.

We posted hair transformations and client testimonials every day, sometimes twice a day. Stories showed work happening in the salon. The website and regularly updated Google Business Profile helped people discover Black beyond Instagram.

As the salon introduced Korean head spa and facials, we worked on the service introductions and offers. We also supported its pedicure expansion and helped plan the launch communication.

The challenge

Choosing a salon means trusting someone with how you’ll look when you leave.

Our approach

Make the quality of the work easier to judge before someone books, through daily transformations, client testimonials and stories from inside the salon.

How it came together

Black turned two, and bookings kept coming.

For “Black turns 2,” we planned two to three weeks of communication around the anniversary and a 20% service offer. That campaign ran without spending on Google or Meta ads.

The client reported about five to ten bookings a day and a sustained busy period around the campaign.

The first-anniversary giveaway the previous year offered two free haircuts. People entered by tagging friends, and the post received around 100 comments.

Regular proof helped Black build an organic following.

When we started, Black had roughly 700 to 800 followers. By November 2026, the account had around 1,500 organic followers and the salon’s Google rating stood at 4.8/5. We continued posting on Google every day alongside the social content.

The team reported more than ten enquiries on a typical day, including enquiries that became appointments. Client videos and Google reviews gave people direct feedback from others who’d used the services.

The approach worked because people could keep seeing the quality for themselves. Consistency gave that proof time to build trust.
