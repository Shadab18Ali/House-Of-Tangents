Kalinga India

Overview

Kalinga needed stronger dining visibility and more walk-in interest alongside the hotel group’s existing business. We focused on the food and the people behind it, including the chefs.

We created the logo and full menu, then developed the website. We also managed social content and Google presence. Separate Meta campaigns supported the work.

The Chicken IFA promotion brought a Dubai-viral food idea into a local campaign, giving people a specific dish to discover.

The challenge

Stronger dining visibility and more walk-in interest alongside the hotel group’s existing business.

The tangent

Let the food and the chefs lead the communication, including a local campaign around a Dubai-viral dish, the Chicken IFA.
