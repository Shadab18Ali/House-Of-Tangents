Majestic India

We built Majestic India from its first public identity.

Status
Delivered
Timeframe
Late October 2025 to August 2026
Sector
Pageant Platform
Scope
Identity, Website, Social media, Launch, Lead campaigns, Event communication

Overview

Majestic India came to us with a founding team and the ambition to build a pageant platform. We created the logo and guidelines, built the website and set up Instagram and Facebook. The business setup and advertising accounts also started with us.

We explored multiple logo routes and discussed how the identity would work in actual use. Once the direction was clear, we developed the reveal and launch communication.

Fresh footage was limited in the early months. We worked with unfinished existing material and turned it into a consistent content presence while the platform took shape.

The challenge

A founding team with the ambition to build a pageant platform, a limited amount of fresh footage, and no public identity yet.

The tangent

Film the logo reveal with blank paper before the final logo existed, then add the completed identity through masking in post-production.

Execution and results

We made room for different chapters in a woman’s life.

Majestic India’s audience included married and separated women, as well as divorced and widowed women. We developed communication that made participation feel possible across those life stages.

A published Women’s Day shoot explored that idea. We also created an AI transformation film that used face morphing to move between women at different stages of life.

The logo reveal needed another kind of solution. The shoot had to happen before the final logo was ready, so we filmed with blank paper and added the completed identity through masking in post-production.

Note: The campaign and reveal work were completed. The transformation sequence used AI-generated imagery.

The work carried through to the coronation.

We handled scripting, shoots, editing and publishing. We also ran contestant lead campaigns and created interactive polls and quizzes. The website and sponsorship decks formed part of the wider communication work.

Our work included coronation communication and press releases, alongside digital/event assets and sponsor videos. The founder secured the sponsors. We created their communication, including material for Ramada by Wyndham, and content for six candidates preparing to represent India internationally.

The new platform began reaching interested contestants.

Contestant lead campaigns ran for around three to five months. Registrations included candidates who later represented India internationally.

We also worked on the quality of the audience. We reviewed the follower base and removed inactive accounts where possible, while making the content more relevant to the people Majestic India wanted to reach.
