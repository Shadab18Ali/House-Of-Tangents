Introduction

Santhal Strikers needed a new identity and social presence for JT20. An older verified account with the same name was still online, so we also had to establish which page represented the current team.

We had roughly two days before play began. We created the accounts and branding, including the line “Santhal ka dum. Jeetenge hum.”

During matchdays, we worked from the stadium and lounge to create player content and timely updates. The communication continued for about a month after the tournament ended.

The challenge

A new identity and social presence were needed within roughly two days, while an older verified account with the same name was still online.

Our approach

Turn a conversation with cricketer Pratyush Kumar into a film about the dream that brought him to the game.

How it came together

Pratyush Kumar’s story gave people someone to follow.

A conversation with Pratyush Kumar gave us the basis for a player film. He was a young cricketer from a village, carrying a dream that many children recognise. We wanted the audience to understand the person behind the name.

The film opened with the familiar question about what a child wants to become. It followed that thought from a small ground and a tennis ball to the ambition of playing cricket.

Other player introductions and personal stories appeared alongside humour and audience games. “This or that” exchanges and emoji-based player guessing gave the team more ways to connect.

The new page found an audience during the tournament.

Our scope covered strategy and account management, player shoots, match coverage, editing, sponsor communication, advertising support, print/stadium assets and X/Twitter updates. Live match statistics and strategic-timeout content needed the same attention as the larger films.

We stayed involved after the final match and continued the team’s post-tournament communication.
