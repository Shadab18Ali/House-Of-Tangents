Hospitality and discovery

Hospitality/Delivered

Capitol Hill

The starting point
A 22-year-old hotel with regular guests and local trust, but a digital presence that didn’t reflect enough of what people experienced there.
The decision we made
Show the food, ambience, service and events people were already experiencing, and give each outlet and the new Aura rooftop a clearer place online.
What our team delivered
Hospitality strategy, Content, Creative, Campaigns, Google Business Profile.

The reported response

  1. 800%+

    organic account reach growth

    Within the first two months · Organic · Source: supplied case study

  2. Up to 400%

    increase in taps on the business address

    After approximately 2.5 months · Source: supplied case study

  3. 2,873

    Google Business Profile interactions, up 54.2% against the previous month

    One reporting period · Source: supplied case study

  4. 5,363

    Google Business Profile interactions, up 25% against a separate comparison period

    A different reporting period · Source: supplied case study

Context: The two Google Business Profile figures come from different reporting periods. Address taps and profile interactions are not confirmed visits or bookings.

Hospitality

Hotel AVN Grand, Kalinga India and The Lobby Café

The starting point
An established hotel with business bookings needed its dining and café offerings to be found. Kalinga India opened in August 2025 and The Lobby Café in 2026.
The decision we made
Let the food and the people behind it lead, and give each venue its own introduction while keeping the link to the hotel clear.
What our team delivered
Social communication, shoots and editing, websites, Google presence and separate Meta campaigns, with original logos and menus for Kalinga India and The Lobby Café.

The reported response

  • Visibility and walk-in improvement were reported, without confirmed totals.
  • Kalinga India’s Valentine’s Day activity received strong bookings. No numerical booking data was supplied.

Context: These outcomes are qualitative. We haven’t converted them into numbers.

Pageants and personal branding

Pageant platform/Delivered

Majestic India

The starting point
A founding team with the ambition to build a pageant platform, a limited amount of fresh footage, and no public identity yet.
The decision we made
Start with the identity and the accounts, keep publishing from the footage that existed, and plan around what wasn’t ready yet. The logo reveal was filmed with blank paper before the final logo existed.
What our team delivered
Identity, Website, Social media, Launch, Lead campaigns, Event communication.

The reported response

  1. ~300

    qualified enquiries from contestant lead campaigns

    Over roughly three to five months · Paid lead campaigns · Source: team record

  2. 700K–800K

    views on selected reels

    Organic. Some reels were collaborations and others were independent. · Source: team record

  3. ~1,400 to 1,500

    page followers

    Source: team record

Context: Paid lead generation and organic reel views are separate results. The life-stages transformation film used AI-generated imagery.
