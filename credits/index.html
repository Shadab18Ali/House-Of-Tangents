How to read the work on this site.

Experience and scope

This site presents selected collective team experience across client work, product engagements and earlier agency roles. Contributions and scope vary by project.

Results

Project results come from team records, supplied case studies and client-reported feedback. Reporting periods are stated where available. Historical results are not guarantees of future performance.

Each figure sits beside its project, with its period, its paid or organic basis and its source where those are recorded. Views, followers, enquiries, address taps and profile interactions are reported as what they are. They aren’t presented as sales or confirmed visits.

Project status

Statuses are a snapshot from the November 2026 project record. Work described as ongoing or pending was ongoing or pending in that record, and may have moved on since.

The film reference

The tangent and perpendicular reference comes from a scene in Gangs of Wasseypur Part II, spoken by the character Guddu. The dialogue shown on this site is a brief excerpt from the published dubbing script, pages 42 to 43. What we’ve made of it is our own interpretation, not the film’s message.

House of Tangents has no association with the film, its makers or its rights holders, and claims no endorsement or ownership of their material. This site uses no film footage, stills, posters or soundtrack.

Sources

Earlier project records appear on the Brand Thinker website. Capitol Hill material appears in the earlier project portfolio and the digital-presence case study. The earlier Black and ZOI cases provide additional project context.

Earlier product conversations

DIM12/DIM-12 and RadiologyGPT appear in earlier product and ecosystem discussions, alongside Pi Social, Pi Learn, Pi Path and Pi Enroll. They were discussions, not delivered client case studies.
