About

Our name has a story behind it.

Tangent thinking. Perpendicular commitment.

Wasseypur made geometry hard to forget.

“अगर जान से मरा, तो ब्लेड परपेंडीकुलर गया था…और अगर सिर्फ चमड़ी कटा, तो समझो टेंजेण्ट था.”

Guddu, Gangs of Wasseypur Part II

Guddu’s dialogue made the angle matter. We’ve given that thought a different job at House of Tangents. Our creative platform is “Tangent thinking. Perpendicular commitment.” We look for an approach people haven’t seen coming, then stay with the work it takes to produce it.

The dialogue is a brief excerpt from the published Gangs of Wasseypur Part II dubbing script, pp. 42–43. Our agency interpretation is our own. This website uses no film footage, stills or other film assets and does not claim or imply any endorsement, affiliation or collaboration with the film or its rights holders.

We think in tangents.

For Navdurga, we imagined a visitor’s journey before there was a finished business park to film. For Santhal Strikers, a conversation with a player became a film about the dream that brought him to cricket.

We commit to the execution.

An idea still needs someone to plan the shoot and write the script. It needs prompt writing, footage selection, editing, design and a publishing plan. We work through those details, including the less visible ones.

The team

We share the work across a four-person team.

Farha Fatima

Founder, Brand & Creative Director

Farha’s background in technology consulting and sales shapes how she approaches a marketing brief. She leads client conversations and pitches. She also sets the creative direction and oversees delivery, while staying involved in shoots and graphic design. She works on campaigns too.

Her earlier leadership experience includes a 12-person team managing 11 client accounts at the same time.

Tanushree

Marketing & Communications Director

Tanushree works across performance marketing and brand communication. She helps turn a campaign idea into communication that makes sense to its audience, then stays involved in everyday execution.

She and Farha work together on graphic design and advertising, keeping the message consistent as it moves from a creative into a client or customer conversation.

Ashish Sahu

Film & Production Director

Ashish works on shoots and follows the footage through post-production. He helps decide what we need to capture, then works on selection, pacing, sound and editing.

That can mean building energy into a campaign film or giving a quieter moment enough room. His involvement runs from the set to the final cut.

Rahul Kumar

Digital Experience Director

Rahul builds websites and helps work out how people will use them. He also contributes research and shoot preparation, and joins the team on set.

He helps gather the material a concept needs, then works with the team to turn the communication into a digital experience people can find their way through.

Our core team has four people. We work with specialist production collaborators when a project calls for them. The projects here represent collective work, and team members and collaborators contributed according to each engagement’s scope.

Behind the HOT

A few seconds on screen can contain days of work.

The script comes after research. A shoot depends on preparation, and a useful edit starts with footage selection. We review the work before it goes out.

Our team crosses between roles because a brief rarely divides itself neatly into departments. We stay involved in the parts the work needs.

We choose the approach by understanding the brief.

We look at what the business needs and who it wants to reach, then assess the material already available. That helps us decide whether the useful next step is an identity or a story, a digital journey or a more consistent content rhythm.

We plan production and build the assets, then publish or hand over the work and review the response available to us. Feedback informs the next version.
