About

A small team with a wider view of the work.

House of Tangents brings together people who think about the business and stay involved in making the work. We research and write, build and shoot, then work through the details that help an idea make sense outside the meeting.

How the House started

A business problem first. Then the communication.

Farha Fatima founded House of Tangents with experience across technology consulting, sales and marketing. That background shapes how we connect a business problem with communication people can understand. Our wider experience includes client work and product engagements, as well as earlier agency roles.

The team

Four people. Shared responsibility for the work.

Farha Fatima

Founder, Brand & Creative Director

Farha leads client conversations and creative direction, staying involved in shoots and design as well as campaign delivery. Her background in technology consulting and sales helps her connect the creative work with what the business is trying to achieve.

Tanushree

Marketing & Communications Director

Tanushree works across performance marketing and brand communication. She helps shape the message and stays involved as it moves into campaign execution. She also works with Farha on graphic design and advertising.

Ashish Sahu

Film & Production Director

Ashish works from shoot preparation through the final cut. He helps decide what the team needs to capture, then shapes the footage through selection and editing, with attention to sound and pacing.

Rahul Kumar

Digital Experience Director

Rahul builds websites and helps work out how people will use them. He also contributes research and shoot preparation, and joins the team on set. His work connects the material behind an idea with the experience people encounter online.

Our core team has four people. Specialist collaborators join according to the project.

How we work

Four stages, in this order.

  1. Understand what needs to change.

    We look at what the business needs and who it wants to reach, then assess the material already available.

  2. Find a useful direction.

    That tells us whether the next step is an identity or a story, a digital journey or a steadier content rhythm.

  3. Make and deliver the work.

    We plan production and build the assets, then publish or hand over the work.

  4. Review the response and improve.

    We review the response available to us. Feedback informs the next version, whether that’s a script or the product experience itself.

This site presents selected collective team experience across client work, product engagements and earlier agency roles. Contributions and scope vary by project.
