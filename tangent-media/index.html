Delivered

Concepts we made and published.

We filmed the reveal before the logo was ready.

Majestic India’s logo was still in development, but the shoot couldn’t wait for the final artwork. We filmed the performance and blank-paper interaction first. Once the logo was complete, we used masking to place it in the footage.

That let production continue while design work was still underway. The reveal campaign was completed and published. Majestic India case study

Majestic India logo reveal

The reveal was filmed with blank paper before the final logo was ready. The completed identity was added through masking in post-production.

We turned the planned site into a visit people could follow.

Navdurga had no completed site to film, so we created an airport-to-location film and a separate AI-generated FPV-style facilities walkthrough. We planned scenes, wrote the prompts, generated the sequences, and added sound and editing.

Both films were published organically on the page we managed. They are AI visualisations of planned facilities, rather than filmed tours of completed construction. Navdurga case study

Proposals

New ideas, clearly labelled.

Nothing below has been produced or published. These are proposed formats based on decisions from our delivered work.

Proposed Proposal — not yet produced

A proposed HOT agency film

The film opens on a screen that says, “Show the business park.” The camera reaches an empty site. A cursor changes the question to, “What would the first visit feel like?” An imagined journey opens up.

A blank sheet then waits for a logo. A cricket scorecard opens into a player’s childhood dream, and a salon chair turns towards a finished haircut. The close returns to the team at the desk: “The angle is the beginning. We stay for the work.”

Production would use team footage, original graphics, approved work samples and labelled AI sequences.

Proposed Proposal — not yet produced

The Brief Took a Turn

A proposed short-form series showing the original brief and the assumption that limited it, followed by the alternative question and finished work.

The first episode could use Navdurga. Later episodes could cover Majestic’s reveal or Pratyush’s story. Black’s decision to keep the approach simple would be another useful episode.

Proposed Proposal — not yet produced

Angle / Execution

A proposed carousel or website format that places an observation beside a production plan: the scene and production method, the assets, and the intended audience response.

We could use the same approach in proposals so a new idea arrives with a clear route into production.
