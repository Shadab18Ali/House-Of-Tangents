Delivered stories

01

Navdurga Business Park/Delivered story

The park wasn’t built. We planned the visit.

The brief
Show a planned business park, in a way that felt more interesting than familiar business-park promotion.
What was getting in the way
There was no completed site to film. A list of distances and facilities would have asked viewers to imagine the experience themselves.
The turn in the thinking
We imagined the first visit instead: arriving, then travelling towards the location.
How we made it
We planned the scenes and wrote the prompts, generated the sequences, then brought them together with voiceover, sound and editing.
The finished work
Two films, published organically on the page we managed: the journey towards the site and a separate FPV-style walkthrough of the proposed facilities.Proposed infrastructure visualisation. These are AI-generated scenes, not footage of completed construction.

02

Majestic India/Delivered story

The shoot couldn’t wait for the logo.

The brief
Reveal the identity of a new pageant platform.
What was getting in the way
The logo was still in development, and the shoot couldn’t wait for the final artwork.
The turn in the thinking
We filmed the performance and the blank-paper interaction first.
How we made it
Once the logo was complete, we used masking to place the finished identity into the footage.
The finished work
Production carried on while the design work was still underway. The reveal campaign was completed and published.

03

JT20 / Santhal Strikers/Delivered story

Before the player introduction, we listened.

The brief
Introduce the players of a team whose new account had roughly two days to get ready before the tournament.
What was getting in the way
A name and a match result don’t give people much reason to care about a player.
The turn in the thinking
A conversation with Pratyush Kumar about his village background and his cricket ambition became the basis for a player film.
How we made it
The film opened with the familiar question about what a child wants to become, then followed that thought from a small ground and a tennis ball to the ambition of playing cricket.
The finished work
The story ran alongside other player introductions during the tournament. The case study has the reported response.

04

Black Salon/Delivered story

The decision was to keep showing the work.

The brief
Help people choose a salon, which means trusting someone with how you’ll look when you leave.
What was getting in the way
People couldn’t judge the quality of the work before they booked.
The turn in the thinking
We kept it simple. Regular transformations and client testimonials gave people the evidence they needed, so the thinking was in choosing what to repeat.
How we made it
Hair transformations and testimonials went out every day, sometimes twice a day, with stories from inside the salon and daily Google posts.
The finished work
Creative judgement includes knowing when consistency is the useful answer. The engagement was ongoing in the November 2026 record.

05

Saregama Music / Maavdi BTS/Delivered story

Everyone in the room needed to be in the film.

The brief
Edit a behind-the-scenes film for “Maavdi” from footage supplied by the shooting team.
What was getting in the way
The requirement was that everyone involved in the room had to appear, including the lighting and sound crews whose contributions are easier to miss.
The turn in the thinking
We let the people and the footage carry the edit. Constant cuts or motion graphics would have pulled attention away from them.
How we made it
Inclusive footage selection, then editing, colour, sound and subtitles, with no motion graphics.
The finished work
Post-production is complete, and the shooting team gave outstanding feedback. Management approval and publication were pending in the November 2026 record, so the film isn’t shown here.
