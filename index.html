The Tangent Method

We look for the angle, then work through the details.

  1. 01

    Find the angle

    We look at what the business needs, who it wants to reach and what its audience is used to seeing. Then we look for a useful detail that gives the brief a different way in.

  2. 02

    Make the work

    We plan, write, design, shoot, build, edit and publish, including the less visible details that decide whether an idea makes sense when people meet it.

  3. 03

    Review the response

    We look at the response available to us and let it shape the next version. For FluffyPet, that meant changing the product experience itself.

Why “Tangents”

A line that touches a circle once, then moves on.

The name comes from a scene in Gangs of Wasseypur Part II, where Guddu explains the difference between a perpendicular and a tangent. It stayed with us because it made the angle matter. We’ve given that geometry its own nonviolent creative meaning.

Tangent thinking. Perpendicular commitment.
