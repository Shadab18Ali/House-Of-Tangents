The House

We look for the angle, then work through the details.

We think in tangents.

For Navdurga, we imagined a visitor’s journey before there was a finished business park to film. For Santhal Strikers, a conversation with a player became a film about the dream that brought him to cricket. We look for the detail that gives a familiar brief somewhere else to go.

We commit to the execution.

An idea still needs someone to plan the shoot and write the script. It needs prompt writing, footage selection, editing, design and a publishing plan. We work through those details, including the less visible ones that decide whether the idea makes sense when people encounter it.

Where we start

We can start with what your business has today.

Some clients come to us with a new account. Others have an unfinished app or a property that’s still on a plan. An established business may need its online presence to catch up with the experience it already offers.

House of Tangents works from that starting point. We bring brand strategy, design, content, production, websites and marketing into the same brief. We help decide what the business needs to communicate and how people should encounter it. We also consider what happens when someone responds.

Our experience comes from client work and product engagements, as well as earlier agency roles. We bring what we’ve learned into the House.

The projects in this portfolio represent collective work. Team members and specialist collaborators contributed according to each engagement’s scope.

Behind the HOT

A few seconds on screen can contain days of work.

The script comes after research. A shoot depends on preparation, and a useful edit starts with footage selection. We review the work before it goes out.

This portfolio includes product conversations and on-ground onboarding, live match updates, event collateral, menus, websites, voiceovers, revisions and daily publishing.

Much of that effort stays out of sight when someone encounters the final creative. It still shapes what they see.

Our team crosses between roles because a brief rarely divides itself neatly into departments. We stay involved in the parts the work needs.

We choose the approach by understanding the brief.

We look at what the business needs and who it wants to reach, then assess the material already available.

That helps us decide whether the useful next step is an identity or a story, a digital journey or a more consistent content rhythm.

We plan production and build the assets, then publish or hand over the work and review the response available to us. Feedback informs the next version.

The change may be in a script or, as FluffyPet showed, in the product experience itself.
